v0.4.18 — Keyboard, connection, and history fixes

• Android: the keyboard no longer covers inputs on the connection screens or the new-session dialog.
• Renaming a connection no longer force-disconnects the live conversation.
• A failed slash command keeps your typed text (and explains why) instead of silently erasing it.
• "Load older messages" now pages through history batch-by-batch instead of one giant fetch — much better for long sessions.
• The model chip updates in place when the latest message's model changes mid-conversation.
• Single-choice prompt answers honor your last tap — no more rare "Reply failed" after a quick double-tap.
